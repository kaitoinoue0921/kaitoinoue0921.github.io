大問3 自由英作文模範解答の一例

Learning about different cultures is valuable because it broadens students' perspectives and helps them develop empathy. When students understand diverse traditions and values, they are less likely to hold prejudices and can communicate effectively in a globalized society. To help students appreciate world cultures, schools can take two effective steps. First, they can organize international exchange programs, such as inviting guest speakers from abroad or connecting with sister schools online. Direct interaction allows students to experience cultural differences firsthand. Second, schools can incorporate multicultural themes into regular classes, such as reading world literature in English lessons or cooking international dishes in home economics. Through these practical and engaging activities, students can naturally build respect for people from different backgrounds. In this way, they learn to listen to others and grow into thoughtful global citizens.
（134語）
